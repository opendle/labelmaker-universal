# Labelmaker privacy policy

Effective date: August 30, 2026

Labelmaker does not collect, send, sell, or share personal data.

The application processes label documents, imported images, printer settings,
and printer communication on your device. It uses Bluetooth to find and use a
printer when you ask it to do so. It uses the system file picker to open and
save files that you select. The application does not use analytics, advertising,
tracking, or a remote account service.

Apple can process purchase, download, update, and diagnostic data under its own
privacy policy. Apple can make store or diagnostic reports available to the
developer. These services are separate from the Labelmaker application.

For privacy questions, open a support request in the
[public issue tracker](https://github.com/opendle/labelmaker-universal/issues).
Do not include private or sensitive information in a public issue.
