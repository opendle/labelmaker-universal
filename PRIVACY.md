# Label Maker privacy policy

Effective date: August 30, 2026

Label Maker does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

## Data collection

Label Maker does not use analytics, advertising, user accounts, tracking
cookies, or developer-operated cloud storage. The application does not send
workspaces, labels, printer settings, or usage data to the developer.

## Local files and application data

Label Maker stores workspaces, printer settings, and application preferences
locally on your device. The application accesses a workspace file only when you
create, select, open, or save that file.

Your device backup or synchronization service can copy application data. That
service operates under its own privacy policy.

## Bluetooth

Label Maker uses Bluetooth to find and communicate with supported label
printers. Bluetooth information and print data stay between your device and the
selected printer. The developer does not receive this information.

## External services

Label Maker does not send personal data or application content to the
developer. If you open an external link, your browser and the destination
service operate under their own privacy policies.

Apple can process information when you download, purchase, update, or use Label
Maker through the App Store. Apple controls this processing. See
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Children's privacy

Label Maker does not knowingly collect personal data from children or other
users.

## Source-available software

Label Maker is source-available software. You can inspect its source code to
verify its behavior.

## Policy changes

If the data practices of Label Maker change, this policy will be updated before
the new practice starts. The effective date at the top of this policy will also
change.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the
[Label Maker GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/opendle/labelmaker-universal/issues).
Do not include private or sensitive information in a public issue.
